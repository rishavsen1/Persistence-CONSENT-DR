\section*{Core Research Hypotheses}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]

    \item Local-only decentralized control is insufficient under coupled infrastructure dynamics.

    \item Market-aware coordination improves global efficiency and reduces peak demand.

    \item Learned interaction representations $r_i$ capture latent inter-building dependencies.

    \item The proposed framework generalizes under changing building populations (plug-and-play setting).

    \item The decentralized architecture scales more effectively than centralized MPC approaches.

\end{itemize}


\section*{Main Narrative Direction}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]

    \item Scalable decentralized coordination under coupled infrastructure systems

    \item Learning-based interaction representation for distributed MPC

    \item Plug-and-play generalization under dynamic agent populations

    \item Coordination under non-stationary multi-building dynamics

\end{itemize}
